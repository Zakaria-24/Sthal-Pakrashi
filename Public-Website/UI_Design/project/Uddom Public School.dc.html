About the School

A community of learners in the heart of Chattogram, growing together since 2000.

Uddom Public School was founded on a simple conviction: that every child in our community deserves an education that is rigorous, joyful and rooted in values. Over two decades we have grown from a single building into a vibrant campus — yet our purpose is unchanged. We blend a strong national curriculum with experiential learning, creative expression and genuine care for each student’s wellbeing.

🎯

Our Mission

To nurture confident, compassionate and capable young people through an education that balances academic excellence with character, creativity and community responsibility.

🌟

Our Vision

To be a school where curiosity is celebrated and every learner leaves ready to shape a better, kinder and more inventive world.
